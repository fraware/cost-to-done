Accounting contract

Cost per success equals the full spend across completed trials divided by their verified successes. Expenses from unsuccessful trials are retained. The ratio is undefined if no trial passes. Exact decimal strings avoid floating-point drift in summed charges.

Comparability contract

Rankings are withheld if task sets or per-task replication differ, or billing and rate-card estimates are mixed. A common task verifier and pinned task digest are mandatory. Five or more distinct tasks unlock descriptive task-cluster bootstrap intervals.
